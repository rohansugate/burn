Upload a video. Whisper translates speech to English in your browser, then captions are burned into the picture. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

First run downloads a small speech model (~75 MB). Short clips work best. For YouTube-style links or long files, run the local app.

Most sites block browser downloads (CORS). A direct .mp4 / .webm link may work. Paste-a-page links need the local app.
